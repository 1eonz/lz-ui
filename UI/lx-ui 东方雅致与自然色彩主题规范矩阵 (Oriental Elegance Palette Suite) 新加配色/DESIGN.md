## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes enterprise-grade clarity with the tranquil restraint of classical Eastern natural color palettes. Built for high-throughput cockpit interfaces, complex operational workflows, and data-dense dashboards, it moves past generic corporate blues into subtle, restorative chromatic palettes that alleviate cognitive fatigue during long operational sessions.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on:

- **Neo-Minimal Corporate Structure**: High structural discipline, razor-sharp alignment, and compact spatial efficiency required for operational platforms.
- **Oriental Organic Naturalism**: 7 curated dual-harmony palettes (Celadon, Twilight Mountain, Garnet, Pine Needle, Misty Purple, Bean Sand, Cheese Butter) that pair grounded mineral tones with muted ambient tints, infusing warm tactility and quiet elegance.
- **Glass & Layered Translucency**: Restrained backdrop filtration on surface cards, subtle hairline ghost outlines, and soft multi-stop diffusion shadows that mimic rice paper layers and fine ceramic glaze.

The target audience encompasses knowledge workers, operations specialists, and analysts who demand WCAG 2.2 AA compliant readability without sacrificing visual poise and poetic balance.

## Colors

The core color structure delivers a light, contemplative workspace rooted in high-contrast neutral backgrounds (`#F8F9FF` to `#FFFFFF`) paired with crisp typography tones (`#0B1C30` on-surface). The system accommodates 7 distinctive Oriental Elegance preset themes, each composed of a dominant mineral tone and a grounding ambient secondary:

1. **Celadon & Laurel (青瓷绿 × 月桂米)**: `#3FA796` & `#E8D9B5` — Calming ceramic green balanced with serene botanical cream.
2. **Twilight Mountain & Evening Peach (暮山蓝 × 晚桃粉)**: `#4A5F8C` & `#E8B4A2` — Deep atmospheric dusk blue softened by a dusk floral tint.
3. **Garnet & Almond Cream (石榴红 × 奶杏白)**: `#B5485D` & `#F3E2D1` — Dignified lacquered crimson warmed with neutral milky almond.
4. **Pine Needle & Amber Gold (松针绿 × 琥珀金)**: `#365B4C` & `#D9A441` — Deep evergreen depth matched with luminous resin gold.
5. **Misty Purple & Oatmeal White (雾霭紫 × 燕麦白)**: `#8877C7` & `#F0E7DB` — Ethereal wisteria purple balanced with natural flax neutrality.
6. **Bean Sand Pink & Ink Teal Grey (豆沙粉 × 墨青灰)**: `#C88697` & `#31424D` — Muted cinnabar rose framed by deep slate calligraphy ink.
7. **Cheese Gold & Distant Mountain Cyan (酪黄 × 远山青)**: `#E7C86A` & `#5D7E7A` — Warm parchment yellow anchored by distant misty ridge teal.

### Accessibility & Contrast Principles

- All text interactions against brand primary surfaces strictly observe WCAG 2.2 AA (minimum 4.5:1 ratio for regular body text, 3:1 for large display text and interactive UI borders).
- Lighter secondary shades (such as Laurel Beige `#E8D9B5` or Almond Cream `#F3E2D1`) must serve strictly as surface fills, container backgrounds, or tag badges with darkened foreground text (`#0B1C30` or dark-toned ink variants).
- Semantic alert feedback (Success, Warning, Error, Info) maintains pure clarity independent of the active theme palette.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes systematic numeric scanning, dense multi-column tabular legibility, and effortless screen rendering. Inter anchors the functional core across all western typographic glyphs, paired seamlessly with system-native East Asian geometric grotesques (`PingFang SC`, `Noto Sans SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`).

- **Display & Section Headers**: `headline-xl` (28px) and `headline-lg` (22px) utilize semi-bold weighting (`600`) with tight letter-tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain composure across dashboards without overwhelming viewport real estate.
- **Cockpit Body Text**: `body-md` (14px/22px) serves as the primary workhorse across inputs, tables, descriptions, and data trees, guaranteeing optimal line scanning and visual cadence.
- **Labels & Micro-data**: `label-xs` (11px) and `label-sm` (12px) offer crisp tag labeling, metric metadata indicators, and compact badges without baseline blur.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is driven by an enterprise 12-column fluid grid governed by rigid horizontal baseline increments and flexible screen adaptation:

- **Columns & Margins**: Desktop displays use a 12-column framework with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) outer canvas margins. Tablet layouts condense to 8 columns with 16px margins, and mobile collapses to 4 columns with 12px outer spacing.
- **Dual Density Modes**:
  - _Comfortable (Default)_: Form elements and standard interactive components maintain a 40px control height with `space-md` (12px) inner horizontal padding.
  - _Compact Mode_: High-density tables, filter bars, and toolbars shift to a 32px control baseline with `space-sm` (8px) padding to accommodate deep analytic tables.
- **Rhythm**: Internal card gaps utilize `space-md` (0.75rem) to preserve grouping coherence, while module-level separations scale to `space-xl` (1.5rem).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy blends clean structural container tiers with delicate ambient shadows tinted by the active theme's tonal character:

- **Surface Container Tiers**:
  - `surface-container-lowest` (`#FFFFFF`): Main data cards, modal dialogues, floating panels.
  - `surface-container-low` (`#EFF4FF` or theme ambient wash): Sub-navigation, table headers, and toolbar wells.
  - `surface-container` (`#E5EEFF`): Canvas background and workspace foundation.
- **Ambient Shadow Craft**: Shadows are diffused and softly tinted rather than muddy neutral gray:
  - _Level 0 (Flat Cards)_: 1px border using `outline-variant` (`rgba(193, 198, 215, 0.6)`) with no drop shadow.
  - _Level 1 (Hover / Interactive)_: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 30, 60, 0.06), 0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 30, 60, 0.04)`.
  - _Level 2 (Popovers / Dropdowns)_: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 30, 60, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 30, 60, 0.03)`.
  - _Level 3 (Modals / Drawers)_: `0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(15, 30, 60, 0.14), 0px 4px 12px rgba(15, 30, 60, 0.05)`.
- **Frosted Glass Mode (`glass` preset)**: Employs `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with 85% opacity background fills and a 1px top highlight border tinted to the secondary accent (e.g., `#E8D9B5` or `#F0E7DB` at 30% alpha), evoking fine polished porcelain and translucent silk.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored shape language (`roundedness: 1`, soft geometry) that balances contemporary efficiency with gentle organic contours:

- **Base Components**: Inputs, buttons, segmented controls, and chips carry a baseline radius of `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Cards & Data Containers**: Cards, table enclosures, and inner panels scale to `0.375rem` (6px) or `0.5rem` (8px), matching the subtle rounding seen in classical carved joinery.
- **Pills & Status Indicators**: Badges, status dots, and micro-counters utilize full rounded geometry (`9999px`) to maintain clean separation from rigid data blocks.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary**: Filled with the active theme primary color (e.g., Celadon Green `#3FA796` or Pine Green `#365B4C`), white text, 40px height in comfortable mode (32px compact), `rounded: 4px`. Hover state lightens brightness by 8%; active state depresses by 1px with a 12% darkened overlay.
- **Secondary / Soft**: Background utilizes the secondary theme tint at 12% opacity (e.g., Laurel Beige `#E8D9B5` at `rgba(232, 217, 181, 0.25)`) with the theme primary for text and border.
- **Ghost & Link**: Borderless with 8px horizontal padding; hover introduces a faint ambient fill.
- **Focus Rings**: Strict 2px offset solid focus ring in primary theme color ensuring WCAG 2.2 visibility.

### Chips & Badges

- **Theme Preset Badges**: Compact 22px height, 8px padding, pill-shaped (`9999px`). Background uses secondary pastel fill with darkened mineral foreground text (e.g., Oatmeal White background `#F0E7DB` with Misty Purple `#8877C7` text).
- **Filter Chips**: 28px height, `rounded: 4px`, bordered with subtle `outline-variant`. Selected state fills with primary brand tint and displays a miniature active checkmark.

### Inputs & Select Fields

- **Container**: White fill (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid border (`#C1C6D7`), transition on border-color over 150ms.
- **States**: Hover switches border to `outline` (`#727786`). Focus transitions border to the active theme primary with a 3px diffused outer halo (`rgba(primary, 0.18)`).
- **Labels**: Top-aligned or floating inside compact cells, using `label-xs` in `on-surface-variant` (`#414755`).

### Data Tables & Lists

- **Header Row**: 36px height, subtle `surface-container-low` background, column labels styled with `label-xs` uppercase tracking.
- **Data Rows**: 44px comfortable (34px compact), 1px solid divider line (`#EFF4FF`). Hover state displays a subtle theme-tinted wash (2% primary alpha). Selected rows show a 3px left vertical accent bar in the primary theme color.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls

- **Checkboxes**: 16x16px square with 2px radius. Unchecked state has a 1.5px border. Checked state transitions to full primary fill with a crisp white checkmark vector.
- **Radio Buttons**: 16x16px circle with a concentric 8px solid center dot on active selection.

### Cards & Dashboards Panels

- Clean white background, 1px perimeter border (`#E5EEFF`), and Level 0/1 ambient shadow.
- Header bars feature subtle hairline bottom dividers and optional secondary accent pills to designate operational contexts or theme-driven category groupings.
